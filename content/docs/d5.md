**Baseline:** 19 November 2026 · **Method:** monitor decisions, exposure and measured continuity; no scenario probabilities estimated

## Watch rules

Each entry distinguishes **Fact** (published status or committed date), **Judgement** (what a change would mean), and **Scenario** (a possible disruption). Dates marked **estimated** are publication-pattern assumptions, not announced deadlines. A positive signal counts only when it reports an observed or exercised service outcome; an announcement alone is a process milestone. Review monthly and immediately after an incident or official decision. Source IDs refer to the accompanying D3 catalogue and F IDs to its findings register; direct links and locators remain here.

## Decision calendar

**Window:** 2026–27, rolling **Status and source: Fact:** AGCMF v4.2 issued Sep 2026 ([S17/F21, PM&C version table](https://www.pmc.gov.au/resources/australian-government-crisis-management-framework-agcmf/version-control)); [ANAO S16/F18–F20, paras 3.68–3.105](https://www.anao.gov.au/work/performance-audit/australian-government-crisis-management-framework) is a 2024 baseline. **Signal to record, including counterevidence:** Publication of dated national exercise selection criteria, independent evaluation, measured service restoration and action closure; record absent results as “not publicly identified,” not “not done.” **Decision use:** Refresh national assurance position.

**Window:** 2026–27, rolling **Status and source: Fact:** [S14/F15 NEMA](https://www.nema.gov.au/about-us/media-centre/ausalert-now-operational) says AusAlert operational (page undated); S48 AusAlert site says ready for use from 1 Oct 2026; [S15 preliminary test](https://www.nema.gov.au/about-us/media-centre/ausalert-national-test-success) reached 94% of mobile cells. **Signal to record, including counterevidence:** Verified handset delivery and performance during degraded network/power; note successful tests as positive evidence and scope of any outage. **Decision use:** Separate rollout from resilient delivery.

**Window:** 2026–27, rolling **Status and source: Fact:** [S19–S20/F23 NEMA proposal](https://www.nema.gov.au/our-work/disaster-recovery/disaster-recovery-funding-framework) and [5 Nov ministers’ communiqué, pp. 1–2](https://www.nema.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-11/Final%20Communique%20-%20NEMMM%205%20November%202026.pdf) still described recovery-funding reform as in development. **Signal to record, including counterevidence:** Formal decision, instrument, commencement and evaluation framework; continue to record existing arrangements until replaced. **Decision use:** Avoid counting a proposal as implementation.

**Window:** Around Nov 2027 and 2028, **estimated** **Status and source:** [S01/F01–F03 CISC 2026 review, pp. 4–17](https://www.cisc.gov.au/resources-subsite/Documents/critical-infrastructure-annual-risk-review-2026.pdf) provides a qualitative risk baseline. **Signal to record, including counterevidence:** Empirical interdependency evidence, completed control assurance, new incident pathways; mark qualitative ranking as judgement, not probability. **Decision use:** Revise map edges and priorities.

**Window:** Annually, publication date **estimated** **Status and source:** [S04/F05 ASD 2025–26 CI chapter](https://www.cyber.gov.au/publication/annual-cyber-threat-report-2025-26/australian-cyber-threat-landscape/critical-infrastructure) recorded 154 incident responses. **Signal to record, including counterevidence:** Sector-specific disruption duration and attack mechanisms with reporting denominators; falling raw counts alone do not prove lower risk. **Decision use:** Refresh cyber exposure, not event odds.

**Window:** End 2028, **published preparation milestone** **Status and source:** [S42/F43 APRA November 2026 outlook, quantum section](https://www.apra.gov.au/news-and-publications/apras-system-risk-outlook-november-2026) reports ASD’s recommended start of critical-system post-quantum implementation by end 2028. **Signal to record, including counterevidence:** Inventory, transition plans, supplier dependencies and implementation start. **Decision use:** Track long-run control readiness; no claim of a 2029 quantum attack.

**Window:** 2027–29, rolling **Status and source:** [S32/RBA FSR §4.1](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/fsr/2026/oct/focus-topic-operational-risk-and-financial-stability.html) and S46 APRA stress test (three-working-day provider outage) describe common providers and a settlement stress test; [S34/F36 RITS assessment §3.4](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/rits/self-assessments/2026/pdf/2026-assessment-rits.pdf) identifies procedural weaknesses. **Signal to record, including counterevidence:** Payments Crisis Coordination Framework publication if confirmed, exercises, settlement incident reports, closure of RITS actions and [S35/F37 quarterly availability](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/rits/system-availability-statistics/2026/). **Decision use:** Test whether payment continuity improves beyond planning.

**Unverified dates.** The proposed 29 January 2027 parliamentary submission deadline, exact 10 June 2027/2028 CIRMP transition dates, UK strategy timing and New Zealand fuel-plan dates are excluded from the decision calendar until their official pages or rules are checked. They should not trigger a “missed deadline” finding. [CISC regulatory obligations](https://www.cisc.gov.au/how-we-support-industry/regulatory-obligations) confirms categories and commencement at a general level; an entity must check its applicable rule and transitional provision.

## Scenario indicators and escalation

**Scenario, probability \*\*not estimated\*\*: S1: carrier core failure affects emergency calls** **Leading indicators and source locator:** Timing ownership, 24-hour alarms, call-completion traces, change controls; [S09–S11/F10–F12 Telstra review, pp. 2–10](https://www.telstra.com.au/content/dam/tcom/dynamic-media-projects/luke-campbell/TAPFindingsforTelstraOutage.pdf); [S38/F40 ACMA emergency-call rules](https://www.acma.gov.au/access-emergency-call-service-during-network-outages-faqs-industry). **Escalate when:** Any nationally material emergency-call access loss or official finding of repeated uncorrected core weakness. **Positive or negative evidence to seek:** Independent fallback test and observed completion; failed camp-on or delayed detection. No assumed EFTPOS/rail effect.

**Scenario, probability \*\*not estimated\*\*: S2: shared cloud/identity loss** **Leading indicators and source locator:** Provider concentration and fourth-party opacity; [S32 RBA FSR §4.1](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/fsr/2026/oct/focus-topic-operational-risk-and-financial-stability.html); [S01/F02 CISC, pp. 10–13](https://www.cisc.gov.au/resources-subsite/Documents/critical-infrastructure-annual-risk-review-2026.pdf). **Escalate when:** Verified multi-entity Australian critical-operation degradation or a failed joint exercise. **Positive or negative evidence to seek:** Measured alternate capacity and customer service, versus fallback sharing identity/DNS.

**Scenario, probability \*\*not estimated\*\*: S3: fuel supply and local distribution shock** **Leading indicators and source locator:** Stockholding by product, arrivals, regional availability and government escalation; [DCCEEW petroleum statistics](https://www.energy.gov.au/energy-data/australian-petroleum-statistics); [S06/F08 government response](https://www.energy.gov.au/news/securing-australias-fuel-supply). **Escalate when:** Official fuel-plan escalation or verified inability to supply priority essential users; no unsourced Brent threshold. **Positive or negative evidence to seek:** Delivered fuel and generator runtime, not reserve target alone; [S01/F03 CISC p. 16](https://www.cisc.gov.au/resources-subsite/Documents/critical-infrastructure-annual-risk-review-2026.pdf) noted mainly cost effects in 2026.

**Scenario, probability \*\*not estimated\*\*: S4: disruptive cyber activity** **Leading indicators and source locator:** ASD/CISC advisories with named target and observed service effect; [S04/F05 ASD CI chapter](https://www.cyber.gov.au/publication/annual-cyber-threat-report-2025-26/australian-cyber-threat-landscape/critical-infrastructure). **Escalate when:** Official active-disruption advisory or demonstrated multi-sector effect. **Positive or negative evidence to seek:** Restoration tests and independent verification; do not infer state sponsorship from generic counts.

**Scenario, probability \*\*not estimated\*\*: S5: weather plus local grid/mobile loss** **Leading indicators and source locator:** Seasonal outlook, AEMO notices, site backup testing and flood-gauge telemetry; [S41/F42 NCRA intro pp. 1–2](https://www.dcceew.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/81097.pdf); [S24–S25/F16–F17 flood records](https://www.nema.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-08/Characterisation%20of%20the%202022%20floods%20in%20the%20Northern%20Rivers%20region.pdf). **Escalate when:** Co-located warning and communications degradation during a declared event. **Positive or negative evidence to seek:** Local outage evidence; NEM-wide no-gap forecast is not a local storm guarantee.

**Scenario, probability \*\*not estimated\*\*: S6: payments/settlement interruption** **Leading indicators and source locator:** Certificate hygiene, RITS notices, quarterly availability, provider concentration; [S33/F35 RBA incident pp. 2–7](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/rits/system-availability-statistics/2026/pdf/payments-settlement-outage-20260127.pdf). **Escalate when:** Critical payment operation outside tolerance or settlement interruption during liquidity stress. **Positive or negative evidence to seek:** FSS continuity, backlog reconciliation, measured liquidity effect. Separate incident from stress-test scenario.

**Scenario, probability \*\*not estimated\*\*: S7: cable/landing disruption** **Leading indicators and source locator:** Verified cable faults, route diversity, repair time; [S01 CISC 2026, pp. 19, 24](https://www.cisc.gov.au/resources-subsite/Documents/critical-infrastructure-annual-risk-review-2026.pdf) uses scenario framing. **Escalate when:** Confirmed Australian capacity loss affecting critical services. **Positive or negative evidence to seek:** Route-level and service-level measurements; public landing concentration evidence is thin.

**Scenario, probability \*\*not estimated\*\*: S8: emergency-call reform transition** **Leading indicators and source locator:** Official decision, legislation, migration plan and cutover tests; [S38 ACMA emergency-call guidance](https://www.acma.gov.au/access-emergency-call-service-during-network-outages-faqs-industry). **Escalate when:** Announced transfer or new legal duties with a cutover date. **Positive or negative evidence to seek:** Parallel operation and rollback evidence; Senate recommendation alone is not implementation.

**Judgement:** Review the watch after any trigger, then preserve the original baseline and log the changed evidence, source date and confidence. The scenarios are decision aids grounded in cited mechanisms. No high/moderate/low likelihood labels are retained because the reviewed sources provide no calibrated 12–36 month probabilities.
