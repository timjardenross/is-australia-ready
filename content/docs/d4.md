## How to read the map

**Fact** means a documented event or official finding. **Judgement** means an assessment or inference. **Scenario** means a plausible test pathway without an observed end-to-end Australian event. Edge tags are **O** (observed incident), **A** (official assessment or requirement), and **S** (scenario). A line shows functional dependence, not the probability, duration or magnitude of failure. The map deliberately omits a Telstra→EFTPOS or Telstra→rail incident edge: the reviewed [Telstra external report, pp. 2–10](https://www.telstra.com.au/content/dam/tcom/dynamic-media-projects/luke-campbell/TAPFindingsforTelstraOutage.pdf) does not establish those specific outcomes.

## Edge register and evidence

**Edge:** O1, O2 **Status and meaning: Fact:** 8 July 2026 timing fault propagated through Telstra network, affecting voice/data and some emergency calls. **Direct source locator:** [Technology Audit Partners, PDF pp. 2–5](https://www.telstra.com.au/content/dam/tcom/dynamic-media-projects/luke-campbell/TAPFindingsforTelstraOutage.pdf). **Limit or counterevidence:** Operator-commissioned external review; [ACMA investigation](https://www.acma.gov.au/acma-statement-telstra-outage) was open in reviewed record. Most postpaid service returned by 12:12 pm.

**Edge:** O3 **Status and meaning: Fact:** 2022 Northern Rivers flood saw multiple gauge failures; communications weaknesses impaired response. **Direct source locator:** [NEMA flood study, printed pp. 65–66](https://www.nema.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-08/Characterisation%20of%20the%202022%20floods%20in%20the%20Northern%20Rivers%20region.pdf); [NSW parliamentary inquiry, para. 3.157](https://www.parliament.nsw.gov.au/lcdocs/inquiries/2866/Report%20No%201%20-%20Response%20to%20major%20flooding%20across%20New%20South%20Wales%20in%202022.pdf). **Limit or counterevidence:** Does not prove every warning failed or that all failures shared one cause.

**Edge:** A1 **Status and meaning: Judgement:** telecom sites rely on electricity and backup power; flood-exposed support equipment is a specific vulnerability. **Direct source locator:** [NSW flood inquiry, para. 3.157 and telecom recommendations](https://www.parliament.nsw.gov.au/lcdocs/inquiries/2866/Report%20No%201%20-%20Response%20to%20major%20flooding%20across%20New%20South%20Wales%20in%202022.pdf). **Limit or counterevidence:** No national site-runtime distribution in reviewed sources.

**Edge:** A2 **Status and meaning: Fact/Judgement:** fuel is needed for freight, farming and essential services; government responded to 2026 supply pressure. **Direct source locator:** [DCCEEW fuel response, “Strengthening Australia’s fuel resilience”](https://www.energy.gov.au/news/securing-australias-fuel-supply); [PM&C taskforce](https://www.pmc.gov.au/domestic-policy/fuel-supply-taskforce). **Limit or counterevidence:** [CISC 2026, p. 16](https://www.cisc.gov.au/resources-subsite/Documents/critical-infrastructure-annual-risk-review-2026.pdf) says Australian effects of the 2026 petroleum shock were mainly cost effects to date.

**Edge:** A3, A4 **Status and meaning: Judgement:** shared cloud/digital suppliers can connect banking and other sectors to common faults. **Direct source locator:** [RBA FSR Oct 2026, §4.1 “Concentration and lack of substitutability”](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/fsr/2026/oct/focus-topic-operational-risk-and-financial-stability.html); [CISC review 2026, pp. 7, 10–13](https://www.cisc.gov.au/resources-subsite/Documents/critical-infrastructure-annual-risk-review-2026.pdf). **Limit or counterevidence:** Exact cross-sector provider overlap and failover capacity are not published in reviewed sources.

**Edge:** A5 **Status and meaning: Fact/requirement:** payments are an ADI critical-operation minimum unless justified otherwise; provider interruption can stop payment sending. **Direct source locator:** [APRA CPS 230, paras 34–37](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-230); [RBA FSR §4.1](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/fsr/2026/oct/focus-topic-operational-risk-and-financial-stability.html). **Limit or counterevidence:** Requirement and risk channel do not prove all banks would fail together.

**Edge:** O4 **Status and meaning: Fact:** expired intermediate certificate disrupted RITS file transfer, LVCS/LVSS and property settlement on 27 January 2026. **Direct source locator:** [RBA incident report, pp. 2–6](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/rits/system-availability-statistics/2026/pdf/payments-settlement-outage-20260127.pdf). **Limit or counterevidence:** FSS settlement continued; separate duplicate-batch issue delayed further property settlements.

**Edge:** A6 **Status and meaning: Fact:** unsafe water incidents require public-health notification; flood-contact produce can be unsafe. **Direct source locator:** [NSW Health response protocol, response criteria](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/environment/water/Pages/nswhrp-microbiological.aspx); [FSANZ flood guidance](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety-horticulture/weather-events). **Limit or counterevidence:** Mechanism, not a claim of national food or water collapse.

**Edge:** S1–S3 **Status and meaning: Scenario:** extended local grid/fuel disruption outlasts telecom backup and impairs warnings, health logistics or freight. **Direct source locator:** Mechanism from [NSW flood inquiry, para. 3.157](https://www.parliament.nsw.gov.au/lcdocs/inquiries/2866/Report%20No%201%20-%20Response%20to%20major%20flooding%20across%20New%20South%20Wales%20in%202022.pdf) and [DCCEEW fuel response](https://www.energy.gov.au/news/securing-australias-fuel-supply). **Limit or counterevidence:** Joint event, runtime and geographic reach are assumptions.

**Edge:** S4, S5 **Status and meaning: Scenario:** three-working-day common-provider outage blocks debt-securities settlement during liquidity shock, constraining collateral and counterparties. **Direct source locator:** [RBA FSR §4.1, “System Risk Stress Test,” paras 1–5](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/fsr/2026/oct/focus-topic-operational-risk-and-financial-stability.html). **Limit or counterevidence:** APRA stress-test construct, **not** an observed outage; banks in test could use existing Exchange Settlement balances. APRA’s own report (S46) puts the fall in average bank balances at about \$11 billion more than baseline.

## Cascade pathways to test

**1. Carrier timing to emergency calls — observed core, bounded outcome.** The July 2026 Telstra sequence is timing-card fault → core propagation → mobile voice/data degradation → some emergency-call failure ([TAP, pp. 2–5](https://www.telstra.com.au/content/dam/tcom/dynamic-media-projects/luke-campbell/TAPFindingsforTelstraOutage.pdf)). **Judgement:** the hidden critical function was timing and its ownership/monitoring, not simply “telecommunications” as a sector ([TAP, pp. 5–10](https://www.telstra.com.au/content/dam/tcom/dynamic-media-projects/luke-campbell/TAPFindingsforTelstraOutage.pdf)). Test alternate-carrier camp-on, independent call-completion monitoring and recovery to a defined service level. A payment-terminal or rail consequence would need its own incident record, traffic evidence and operator confirmation.

**2. Payments technology to settlement and liquidity — observed technical incident plus separate scenario.** The [RBA’s January 2026 report, pp. 2–7](https://www.rba.gov.au/payments-and-infrastructure/rits/system-availability-statistics/2026/pdf/payments-settlement-outage-20260127.pdf) establishes a certificate and file-transfer dependency and identifies monitoring/contact/playbook shortcomings. The [RBA’s October 2026 stress test](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/fsr/2026/oct/focus-topic-operational-risk-and-financial-stability.html) separately models a three-working-day settlement blockage in liquidity stress (APRA stress test, S46). **Scenario:** a longer outage could constrain collateral movement and counterparties. Do not splice the stress-test liquidity effects into the January incident. Test certificates, duplicate detection, backlog reconciliation, collateral alternatives and communication clocks.

**3. Local weather to warning and response — observed components, compound scenario.** The [Northern Rivers study, pp. 65–66](https://www.nema.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-08/Characterisation%20of%20the%202022%20floods%20in%20the%20Northern%20Rivers%20region.pdf) documented gauge failures; the [NSW inquiry, para. 3.157](https://www.parliament.nsw.gov.au/lcdocs/inquiries/2866/Report%20No%201%20-%20Response%20to%20major%20flooding%20across%20New%20South%20Wales%20in%202022.pdf) documented emergency communications problems and exposed support equipment. **Scenario:** simultaneous loss of grid, site backup and road access would impair warnings and replenishment. [AusAlert’s operational status (ready for use from 1 October 2026 per the AusAlert site, S48; the NEMA page is undated, S14)](https://www.nema.gov.au/about-us/media-centre/ausalert-now-operational) is positive progress, but cell broadcast still depends on a functioning path to the handset.

**4. Fuel to essential logistics — exposure with measured counterweight.** The [March 2026 PM&C taskforce](https://www.pmc.gov.au/domestic-policy/fuel-supply-taskforce) and [DCCEEW response](https://www.energy.gov.au/news/securing-australias-fuel-supply) demonstrate coordination and response. **Scenario:** a prolonged, regionally concentrated diesel shortfall could slow freight, generator refuelling and emergency fleets. Check physical regional stock and distribution against [Australian Petroleum Statistics](https://www.energy.gov.au/energy-data/australian-petroleum-statistics); do not infer local days of cover from national stock or a target. [CISC 2026, p. 16](https://www.cisc.gov.au/resources-subsite/Documents/critical-infrastructure-annual-risk-review-2026.pdf) and the [Productivity Commission’s 2021 study, executive summary](https://www.pc.gov.au/inquiries-and-research/supply-chains/report/) temper universal-collapse claims.

## What would improve the map

**Judgement.** For each critical operation, obtain named provider/function, fourth-party dependency, geography, power and fuel autonomy, alternate route, tested capacity, detection lag, time to degraded service and recovery, and data reconciliation. [CPS 230, paras 27–31 and 34–47](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-230) supplies the regulated-entity basis; [CISC 2026, pp. 4–7](https://www.cisc.gov.au/resources-subsite/Documents/critical-infrastructure-annual-risk-review-2026.pdf) supplies the systemic rationale. Public sources alone cannot score edge strength or national time to impact. No unsupported rail cascade or nationally quantified bank–carrier–cloud overlap is asserted here.
